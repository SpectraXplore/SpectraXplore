Spectral Reference Library

Explore More Than 3,000 Spectral Targets

From minerals and alteration minerals to materials and vegetation, SpectraXplore™ provides a comprehensive spectral reference library for automated and semi-automated mapping — matched against your imagery with Spectral Angle Mapper.

  • Searchable library spanning minerals, alteration products, man-made materials and vegetation
  • Per-pixel similarity scoring with SAM heatmaps and contour visualisation
  • Reference spectra shown alongside your scene for rapid QA/QC

Terrain & Structure

Reveal the Structural Framework

Terrain is more than elevation. SpectraXplore™ transforms DEM data into structural and geomorphological evidence — slope, ruggedness, hillshade, and automatically extracted lineaments — ready to feed directly into your prospectivity model.

  • Slope, aspect, curvature, TRI and multi-directional hillshade derivatives
  • Automatic lineament extraction with density & proximity grids
  • Structural-corridor intersection density for target vectoring
